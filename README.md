# Nassau Candy Product Line Profitability and Margin Performance Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the product profitability and margin performance of Nassau Candy Distributor using Python, Pandas, SQL and Streamlit.

The main objective is to identify profitable and low-margin products, analyze sales and cost performance, evaluate division-wise profitability and provide business recommendations based on data analysis.

An interactive Streamlit dashboard was developed to present the analytical results through visualizations, KPIs and filters.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, cost and gross profit.
- Calculate gross margin and profit per unit.
- Identify high-profit and low-profit products.
- Compare profitability across different divisions and regions.
- Analyze revenue and profit contribution.
- Identify cost-heavy and margin-poor products.
- Perform Pareto analysis to understand profit concentration.
- Explore product and factory relationships.
- Provide data-driven business recommendations.

## 10. Key Findings and Business Recommendations

The analysis helps identify products that generate higher gross profit, products with lower margins and divisions with different profitability performance.

Business recommendations include:

- Review pricing for low-margin products.
- Control product costs.
- Focus on products with strong profit contributions.
- Monitor divisions with lower profitability.
- Reduce excessive dependency on a limited number of products.
- Evaluate factory and product relationships for better operational planning.
